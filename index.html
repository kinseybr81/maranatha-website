What's Happening at Maranatha

Welcome to Maranatha

Maranatha Baptist Fellowship is a Southern Baptist church family in Topeka, Kansas, committed to knowing Christ, growing together, sharing the Gospel, and making disciples.

Set Free • Set Apart • To Harvest

- Our Mission -

To make Jesus KNOWN to people.

Whether you're looking for a church home, exploring your faith, or simply want to learn more about Jesus, we invite you to join us as we worship, study God's Word, and grow together in Christ.

- Our Purpose -

We believe the church is called to equip and train God's people to share the Good News of Jesus Christ. At Maranatha Baptist Fellowship, our desire is to give believers the biblical foundation, encouragement, and practical tools they need to go out and share the Gospel — that Jesus died and rose again to save us from our sin — with the people God places in their everyday lives.

Join us and see how we are being BOLD with the GOSPEL!

Life at Maranatha

Church is more than a Sunday morning. At Maranatha, we grow in Christ together, build lasting relationships, serve our community, invest in the next generation, and encourage one another to share Jesus wherever God has placed us.
